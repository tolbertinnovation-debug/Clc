Who We Are Worldwide

An international Christian communion.

Christ Laborers Worldwide Anglican Church (CLWAC) is an international Christian ecclesiastical and mission-oriented body committed to proclaiming Jesus Christ, advancing discipleship, developing responsible leaders, providing theological and ministry education, serving humanity, promoting Christian unity, recognizing distinguished service, and building peaceful relationships with churches, ministries and communities around the world.

Through its departments, councils, academies, fellowships, orders and service programs, CLWAC equips Christian leaders, strengthens churches, promotes cooperation among Bible-based denominations, supports humanitarian initiatives, encourages interfaith understanding, and provides opportunities for Christian leadership and lifelong learning.

Our Global Vision

A respected international Christian ecclesiastical and service community advancing faith, Christian unity, theological education, leadership development, humanitarian service, peacebuilding, interfaith understanding and recognition of exceptional service.

Our Commitment

A community of faith, learning, service, fellowship and leadership, united around faith, service, education, peace, compassion, integrity and human dignity.

Our Guiding Principles

Ten principles that guide our work.

Our global mission: to proclaim Christ, equip God's people, strengthen Christian leaders, promote unity among believers, provide accessible Christian education, serve humanity, encourage peaceful relationships among religious communities, and recognize lives of exceptional service.

Faith

We remain committed to Jesus Christ and the Christian faith.

Truth

We promote integrity, honesty, transparency and responsible leadership.

Service

We demonstrate our faith through service to God and humanity.

Unity

We encourage cooperation and fellowship among Christians.

Respect

We treat people of different traditions and communities with dignity.

Peace

We promote reconciliation, peaceful coexistence and responsible dialogue.

Education

We equip people with knowledge, wisdom and practical leadership skills.

Excellence

We pursue high standards in ministry, education and service.

Compassion

We stand with vulnerable people and communities.

Accountability

We promote ethical governance and responsible stewardship.

03

Christian Chivalric & Charitable Order

Order of Christ Laborers: Knights & Dames of Christian Service

Dedicated to faith, integrity, leadership, charity, peace, education and humanitarian service, the Order honors men and women who show exceptional commitment to Christian service, leadership, community transformation and humanitarian work.

Members are expected to uphold Christian character, integrity, humility, compassion, peace, responsible leadership and service to humanity.

The Order is a non-military Christian service organization, distinct from historical medieval military orders.

Ranks & distinctions

  1. Grand Patron
