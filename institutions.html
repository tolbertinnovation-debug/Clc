Health

04 · Distance learning

Distance & Correspondence Christian Education for Religious Bodies

Enables churches, ministries and religious organizations to offer structured education to members who cannot attend residential institutions. We provide study materials, examinations, assessments, online instruction, correspondence learning and completion certificates.

  • Bible courses
  • Discipleship programs
  • Leadership training
  • Ministry administration
  • Pastoral development
  • Evangelism & missions
  • Marriage & family
  • Youth ministry
  • Church planting
  • Christian ethics
  • Chaplaincy training
  • Community development
  • Christian leadership

Academic and ministry credentials are identified according to the nature of each program and do not automatically constitute government accreditation, professional licensure or ecclesiastical ordination.
