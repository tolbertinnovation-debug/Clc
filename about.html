Our Story

Called to the harvest.

Since March 10, 2020, Christ Laborers has been a family of ordinary people who answered Jesus' call to let down their nets at His word.

How it began

Born in a season when the world stood still.

Christ Laborers Worldwide Anglican Church of Liberia was established on March 10, 2020, just as the world was about to shut its doors.

While many places went quiet, a small company of believers in Paynesville chose to gather, pray and labor for the Kingdom. We believed that the harvest is plentiful and the laborers are few (Matthew 9:37). We also believed God was raising up workers for Liberia in that very hour.

Six years on, that small gathering has become a vibrant congregation. It has a School of Discipleship, a growing team of ordained clergy, and a calendar marked by revival. Our story is still being written, one faithful laborer at a time.

Our Mission

To make disciples of Jesus Christ who are equipped, sent and faithful laborers in His harvest.

Our Vision

A Spirit-filled, Word-rooted church raising laborers for Liberia and the nations.

Our Emblem

Every part of our logo preaches.

Our emblem shows Luke 5:4–6. Jesus tells tired fishermen to put out into deep water and let down their nets. When they obey, the catch is so great that the nets begin to break.

  • The radiant cross
    Christ is at the center and above everything we do. The light comes from Him.
  • Three laborers, one net
    Red, gold and blue: different people, different gifts, pulling together in unity.
  • The overflowing net
    Souls won for the Kingdom. When we obey His word, the harvest is more than we can carry alone.
  • The ribbon: March 10, 2020
    A reminder of God's faithfulness from our very first day.

Our Core Values

What shapes how we live.

The Word

Scripture is our foundation. We teach it plainly, study it deeply and live it daily.

What We Believe

Rooted in the historic faith.

As an Anglican church we stand with the one, holy, catholic and apostolic faith confessed in the Apostles' and Nicene Creeds.

  1. The Holy Scriptures

    The Bible is the inspired Word of God, containing all things necessary for salvation.

  2. The Holy Trinity

    One God, eternally existing as Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

  3. Jesus Christ

    Fully God and fully man, crucified for our sins, risen bodily, and coming again in glory.

  4. Salvation by Grace

    We are saved by grace through faith in Christ alone, not by our own works.

  5. The Holy Spirit

    The Spirit gives new life, empowers believers with gifts, and sends us out in power.

  6. The Sacraments

    Holy Baptism and Holy Communion, ordained by Christ, are signs and means of His grace.

  7. The Church

    The body of Christ, called to worship, fellowship, make disciples and serve the world.

  8. The Great Commission

    Every believer is called to go and make disciples of all nations (Matthew 28:19–20).
