Every ministry at Christ Laborers exists to help you know Christ, grow in Him and go for Him. There is room in the boat for you.

Equipping The Saints School

Our School of Discipleship, Leadership and Ministry trains believers to follow Jesus faithfully and to lead others to do the same.

  • Weekly classes, Mondays at 4:00 PM
  • Structured training manuals
  • Mentoring from experienced ministers
  • A pathway into leadership and ministry

Revival & Prayer

We believe in seasons of outpouring. Our revival meetings and prayer gatherings create space for God to move in power.

  • Five-day revival meetings each year
  • 50 Days With the Lord season of consecration
  • Midweek prayer and the Word
  • Prayer retreats

Praise & Worship

Our music ministry leads the church in joyful, Spirit-filled praise with vibrant Liberian worship in reverent Anglican order.

  • Choir and praise team
  • Musicians and instrumentalists
  • Worship leaders in training

Clergy & Ministry Formation

We identify, train and ordain those God is calling into pastoral ministry so the church is led by tested, faithful servants.

  • Ordination and commissioning
  • Ongoing training for clergy
  • Pastoral care and accountability

  • Women's fellowship
  • Men's fellowship

Youth & Children

  • Youth fellowship and mentoring
