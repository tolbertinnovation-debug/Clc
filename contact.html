Your First Sunday

What to expect.

A warm welcome

Our ushers will greet you at the door and help you find a seat.

Joyful worship

Expect lively praise, heartfelt prayer and reverent worship.

The Word preached

A clear, practical, Bible-based message for everyday life.

A family

Questions

Good to know.

What should I wear?

Come as you are. Some people dress up in their Sunday best and others come simply. What matters is that you are here.

How long is the service?

Our Sunday Divine Worship Service usually lasts about two and a half hours, including praise, prayer, the Word and Holy Communion.

Is there something for my children?

Yes. Children are welcome in the service, and our Children and Youth Ministries provide Bible teaching for young people.

How do I become a member?

See our membership steps, or speak with a pastor after any service.

How do I find you?

We are in the Woodcamp/Pipeline area of Paynesville. Use the map above, or call +231 779 230 549 and we will guide you in.
