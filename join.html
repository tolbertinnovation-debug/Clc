Join the Christ Laborers Family

There's a place for you in the harvest.

We welcome everyone who sincerely wants to follow Jesus Christ and belong to a community of faith, service, discipleship and fellowship.

Membership Procedure

Six steps to membership.

  1. Attend

    Visit a worship service, Bible study, prayer meeting, ministry program or crusade.

  2. Connect

    Meet a pastor, ministry leader or membership representative, who will counsel you and lead you to Christ if you are not yet saved.

  3. Apply

    Complete the official membership application with the required details.

  4. Orientation

    Take part in the Church's membership and discipleship orientation.

  5. Discipleship

    Begin a structured journey of biblical learning, prayer, fellowship and spiritual growth.

  6. Recognition

    After completing the requirements, be formally recognized as a member according to the Church's constitution and policies.

Membership Expectations

Living as a laborer.

Members are encouraged to:

  • Follow Jesus Christ faithfully
  • Take part in worship and fellowship
  • Study and obey the Word of God
  • Keep holy Christian character in public and private
  • Serve according to their gifts, under the authority of the church
  • Support the mission of the Church
  • Respect Church leadership and policies
  • Take part in evangelism and community service
  • Promote peace, unity and love
  • Grow in faith and live as Christ lived on this earth

Partner With Us

Become part of the Christ Laborers family.

Churches and ministries that share our commitment to Christ, biblical discipleship, evangelism, leadership development, family strengthening, missions and community service are welcome to associate or partner with Christ Laborers Church (WAC).

We welcome

Partnership opportunities

  • Local church partnerships
  • Church planting partnerships
  • Ministry partnerships
  • Mission partnerships
  • Educational partnerships
  • Humanitarian partnerships
  • Community development
  • International church relationships
  • Ministerial fellowship
  • Institutional cooperation

Types of relationships

  • Church partnerships or merging with us: cooperation with Christian churches and ecclesiastical bodies
  • Ministry affiliations: relationships with Christian ministries and missions
  • Educational partnerships: theological schools, Bible colleges and Christian institutions
  • Humanitarian partnerships: joint community and humanitarian initiatives
  • Ecumenical partnerships: Christian unity, dialogue and collaborative service
  • Interfaith relationships: respectful cooperation on peace, humanitarian and community matters
For churches seeking to join

The general process

  1. Submit an expression of interest
  2. Complete the Church/Ministry information form
  3. Provide leadership and organizational information
  4. Take part in an introductory meeting
  5. Review our constitution, policies, doctrine and partnership requirements
  6. Complete the assessment process
  7. Receive an official partnership or affiliation decision

We seek partnerships based on Christ-centered fellowship, mutual respect, integrity, accountability, shared mission and responsible leadership.
