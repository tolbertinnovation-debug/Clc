Shepherds & Servants

Leaders who labor alongside you.

Our clergy and ministry leaders are called, trained and ordained to care for God's people with humility and faithfulness.

Presiding BishopRt. Rev. Dr. Ishmael L. Togbah

Our Spiritual Father

Rt. Rev. Dr. Ishmael L. Togbah

Presiding Bishop of Christ Laborers Worldwide Anglican Church of Liberia and Lead Disciple-Maker of the Equipping The Saints™ School of Discipleship, Leadership and Ministry.

Dr. Togbah has shepherded Christ Laborers since its founding in 2020. He is known for fiery, Scripture-soaked preaching, and just as much for patient, hands-on disciple-making. Every Monday he takes his seat among students with a Bible open, teaching the next generation how to follow Jesus and lead others to do the same.

Under his leadership the church has held multi-day revival meetings, seasons of consecration such as 50 Days With the Lord, and ordination services that have set apart a new generation of ministers.

Our Clergy & Ministers

A team set apart for service.

Men and women who have walked through discipleship and training and have been commissioned by the laying on of hands.

Bishop

Guards the faith, ordains ministers and gives spiritual oversight to the whole church.

Priests & Pastors

Preach the Word, administer the sacraments and shepherd the congregation.

Deacons

Serve the practical needs of the church family and assist in worship.

Ministry Leaders

Lead worship, youth, women, men and outreach, equipping others to serve.

Ordination & Commissioning

From the classroom to the calling.

Leadership at Christ Laborers grows through discipleship. We do not simply appoint leaders. We raise them.

  1. Disciple

    Grow in the Word through Equipping The Saints.

  2. Serve

    Prove faithful in ministry within the church.

  3. Train

    Receive leadership and ministry formation.

  4. Ordain

    Be set apart and sent by the laying on of hands.
