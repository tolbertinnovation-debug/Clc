Questions

Good to know.

What should I wear?

Come as you are. Some people dress up in their Sunday best and others come simply. What matters is that you are here.

How long is the service?

Our Sunday Divine Worship Service usually lasts around two hours, including praise, prayer, the Word and Holy Communion.

Is there something for my children?

Yes. Children are welcome in the service, and we have Bible teaching for children and youth.

How do I find you?

We are at Pipeline Supermarket in Paynesville. Use the map above, or call +231 779 230 549 and we will guide you in.
