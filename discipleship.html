Equipping The Saints™ · Liberia

Grow deep. Go far.

The School of Discipleship, Leadership and Ministry meets every Monday at 4:00 PM. It is open to every believer who wants to know Christ and make Him known.

About the School

“Go ye therefore, and teach all nations…”

Jesus did not ask us to make converts. He asked us to make disciples. Equipping The Saints exists to obey that command.

Led by Bishop Dr. Ishmael L. Togbah, our Lead Disciple-Maker, the school combines Bible teaching, discussion, prayer and practical ministry training. Whether you were saved last week or have followed Jesus for decades, you will be stretched, strengthened and sent.

What You Will Learn

A journey from believer to laborer.

Classes follow the Equipping The Saints training manuals and move through five broad areas of growth.

Foundations of Faith

Salvation, the Word of God, the Trinity and the basics of the Christian life.

Spiritual Disciplines

Prayer, Bible study, fasting, worship and walking in the Spirit.

Christian Character

Holiness, integrity, humility and the fruit of the Spirit in daily life.

Leadership

Servant leadership, stewardship, teamwork and leading by example.

Ministry & Mission

Evangelism, making disciples, spiritual gifts and serving in the church.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Do I need to be a member of Christ Laborers to join?

No. The school is open to every believer, whatever church you attend. Come and grow with us.

How much does it cost?

There are no tuition fees. Students give a willful contribution and pay only for their discipleship training manual.

I missed the first class. Can I still join?

Yes. Classes run every week, so come this Monday. We will help you catch up with your manual.

How can I support the school?

You can become a sponsor to help provide manuals, Bibles and materials for students. Call +231 779 230 549 or visit our Give & Partner page.
